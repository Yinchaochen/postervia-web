Ask someone who has done it

Tell us one thing you need to get done in Berlin. A person looks it up and sends you the answer within 24 hours, with the source and the date we checked it.

What is hard to find

Ask an AI where to register your address and it will tell you. It will not tell you that the guides saying six to eight weeks for a residence permit extension are out of date. The office is taking four to eight months. We checked that one ourselves.

Some of what you need is written down in ten places that disagree. Some of it is not written down anywhere, and the only people who know it are the ones who did it last month.

How it works

  1. You write what you need to get done, and by when.
  2. A person looks it up. You get the official source, the date we checked it, and what people who have already done it say really happens.
  3. You get it back within 24 hours. If we cannot find a solid answer, we say so instead of guessing.

What it costs

Nothing, and nothing later either. We ask for two things back, and you can refuse both.

  • Tell us whether the answer worked.
  • If it worked, write a few lines for the next person with the same question. With your name on it, or without. You choose.

People in Berlin who arrived in the last year, or are about to. Right now one person answers these by hand, so we take a few at a time. If we cannot get to yours, we will tell you rather than leave you waiting.

About your question

What you write goes to us and to nobody else. We ask for a contact address because we cannot send you an answer without one, and we use it for that only. We will not put you on a list or send you advertising. We delete your question and your contact details within three months of answering, or right away if you ask us to. The form is run for us by Tally BV (Ghent, Belgium) on servers in the EU. To load it, your browser sends its IP address to Tally and to Cloudflare, which serves this page; that is technically necessary, we do not store it, and this page sets no cookies. We count page opens and button clicks, without cookies and without anything that identifies you. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: you asked us for something and we are doing it. If you later agree to let us publish what you learned, that is a separate yes and you can take it back at any time.
