About this survey

This survey is anonymous. We do not ask for your name, email address or anything else that identifies you, so please leave such details out of the free-text answers too. The form is run for us by Tally BV (Ghent, Belgium) on servers in the EU. To load it, your browser sends its IP address to Tally and to Cloudflare, which serves this page; that is technically necessary, we do not store it, and this page sets no cookies. We use the answers for product development (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and delete the raw answers twelve months after the survey closes; only summaries remain.
